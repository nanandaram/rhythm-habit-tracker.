## Product Hunt listing

**Tagline (60 characters)**

Build habits around real life, not rigid schedules.

**Description**

Rhythm is a local-first habit tracker for daily routines, flexible weekly goals, reflections, and sustainable momentum. Set “three times this week” goals, update missed days honestly, and keep your data in your control.

**Maker’s first comment**

Hi Product Hunt! I built Rhythm because many habit trackers make a missed day feel like failure. I wanted a gentler system: a goal like “exercise three times this week” should fit around a real week, not force a rigid schedule.

Rhythm includes flexible weekly goals, daily routines, one-line reflections, history editing, and local backups. It is local-first, so your habit data stays in your browser unless you choose to export it.

I would love feedback on two things: does the flexible-goal model feel clearer than a fixed daily streak, and what would make you return tomorrow?
